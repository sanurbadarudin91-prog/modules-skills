# OWASP Top 10 Audit Framework

## Overview
A structured audit approach to evaluate applications against the OWASP Top 10 risks.

## Workflow
1. **Scope**: Identify application boundaries.
2. **Assess**: Perform systematic checks for each of the 10 risks.
3. **Report**: Rank risks (SSVC/CVSS) and document remediation.

## Security Rules
- Review vulnerabilities as a business risk, not just technical bugs.
- Perform audit before every major release.
- Validate remediations with re-testing.
